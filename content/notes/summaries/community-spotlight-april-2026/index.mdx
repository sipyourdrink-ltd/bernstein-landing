## summary

april 2026 was bernstein's first full month as a public project - the span between v1.0.0 and v1.8.8. a handful of contributors shipped work that made the release: the 4,198-line orchestrator broken into 22 sub-packages, the adapter list grown from 7 to 18, the epsilon-greedy bandit router that halved per-session costs, the Cloudflare cloud execution backend, and genuine Windows support with correct env passthrough, Unicode handling, and process management. seven contributors are named; all are in `CONTRIBUTORS.md`.

## key takeaways

- [@oldschoola](https://github.com/oldschoola) landed Windows compatibility across 3 PRs, plus codex config, task filtering, and auto-PR.
- adapter count grew from 7 to 18 in one month; each adapter is roughly 200 lines.
- bandit router cut spend roughly in half on internal self-development runs.
- the project is Apache 2.0 with no CLA; contributor entry points are good-first-issues, adapter adoption, and benchmark submissions.

for contributors or teams evaluating bernstein's community health who want to understand what shipped in the first month post-launch and who drove it.
