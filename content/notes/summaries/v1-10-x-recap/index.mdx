## summary

five point releases between 1.10.1 and 1.10.6 addressed the friction most visible once bernstein was used on a real polyglot codebase. `bernstein agents-md` generates a canonical AAIF AGENTS.md as the single source of truth and rewrites it into four vendor-specific formats (Cursor `.cursor/rules/*.mdc`, Claude Code `CLAUDE.md`, Aider `CONVENTIONS.md`, Goose `.goosehints`) in one `sync` command, with a `verify` CI gate that fails on drift. cost legibility got two patches: a per-turn budget countdown and a `--max-cost-usd` hard cap. A2A v1.0 now ships JWS-signed agent cards at `/.well-known/agent.json` with Ed25519, RFC 8707 audience binding, 24-hour key rotation grace, and a standalone verifier (`tools/verify_audit_dsse.py`) with no bernstein dependency. the adapter count grew from 31 to 44, with notable additions for Devin for Terminal, JetBrains Junie, AWS Q Developer, and DeepSeek V4-Flash/V4-Pro (the DeepSeek adapter ships an EU-residency guard that pins the endpoint host).

## key takeaways

- `bernstein agents-md sync` produces five files in one pass; `bernstein agents-md verify` fails CI on any drift between the canonical IR and the vendor-specific copies.
- `--max-cost-usd` aborts a run cleanly at a hard dollar ceiling and merges or rolls back partial results the same way a normal cancel does.
- the standalone audit verifier asserts that `import bernstein` raises `ModuleNotFoundError` inside its own venv - the property an external auditor wants.
- the Cursor adapter was rewritten from scratch after the previous version shelled a non-existent binary; the new version has 242 lines of contract tests targeting the real `cursor-agent` CLI flags.

## when to read this

for operators upgrading from 1.10.0 who want to know what changed in the patch releases, or for teams evaluating AGENTS.md synchronization, A2A compliance, or the new adapter additions.
